guide

1. Make the first nails
Press make nail by hand. Make 100 nails to see what comes next.

2. Build a rhythm
After 100 nails, you most likely ran out of iron. Buy 100g of iron to keep making nails until the machinery chapter opens at 250 nails. Buy nail makers when you can: they turn iron into nails without another click.

3. Put the ground to work
Buy a nail breaker. It spends nails to mine iron and copper from the map. Keep an eye on the deposits, and move to the next map once the current one is empty.

4. Tune the workshop
Use the tuning switches when you need to pause a system. Factories use copper over time and can be leaned toward makers or breakers.

5. Find balance
Once Yin and Yang appear, let the two sides develop. Watch which side is ahead, use the live bonus, and try to fill both bars together.

6. Complete a life
A full Yin/Yang cycle earns Tao. Tao earns karma when you reincarnate. Reincarnation keeps your long-term progress, but starts the next life with a fresh economy.

7. Keep the loop going
Save locally or sign in for cloud saving and the global leaderboard. End a run for its score, or reincarnate when you want the next life to begin stronger.

Making and selling
Making one nail consumes 1 g of iron and adds one unsold nail. Unsold nails sell automatically. Price changes demand: higher prices earn more per nail but reduce how many customers buy. Marketing raises demand multiplicatively, and each level costs more than the last.

Machinery
Nail makers consume iron and produce nails every second. Nail breakers consume unsold nails as ammunition, then mine every active iron and copper deposit once per nail. Factories complete one unit per cycle, with the balance slider deciding whether that unit is a maker or breaker.

The map
Each map has a fixed pattern of iron and copper deposits. Deposits have more capacity on later maps. A breaker can only mine while it has nails to spend and while deposits remain. The map health readout shows remaining material.

Tuning and rates
The switches stop consumption and production for their system. The readouts show theoretical rates, while actual production is limited by available iron, nails, copper, demand, and remaining deposits.

Yin, Yang, and Tao
Yin rises when harvesting is ahead of use. Yang rises when use is ahead of harvesting. Balance gives a live production bonus. Filling both bars together awards Tao and resets the bars. Tao also increases demand, while Tao's current-life rate bonus does not survive reincarnation.

Reincarnation and saving
Reincarnation converts Tao into permanent karma and rolls independent starting bonuses for the next life. Karma improves the odds of stronger starts. The map, Yin/Yang progress, unlocks, and long-term progression persist; the economy is rebuilt from the new starting package. Local saves use this browser, while signed-in players also receive cloud autosaves.
